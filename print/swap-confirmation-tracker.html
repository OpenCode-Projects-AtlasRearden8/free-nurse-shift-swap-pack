Swap-Confirmation Tracker Cards

One card per swap. The magic combo is kind ask → written yes → approved → tracked — this card is the “tracked” part, so nobody ever relives “wait, I thought you covered me?”

The Pocket Version (fits behind a badge, if your badge allows)

+--------------------------------+
|  SWAP: [__ /__] <-> [__ /__]     |

What each line means

  • Agrees in writing — a text or email that states both dates out loud.
  • Coverage math — the three checks from the Coverage Math One-Pager.
  • Manager approval (written) — not “they said it's probably fine.”
  • Schedule shows the change — the schedule itself is the only source of truth.
  • Coupons — from the Thank-You Coupons sheet, redeemable by heroes.

Two tips that save friendships

  1. Reminders are kindness, not distrust. “See you tomorrow for our swap — thanks again!” protects both calendars.
  2. Close the loop out loud. When their date arrives and everything holds, tell them it went well. Closed loops get traded with again.
