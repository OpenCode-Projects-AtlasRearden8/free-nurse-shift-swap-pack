# Unit-Approval Checklist (Your Swap Paper Trail)

*Education and organization only — this pack is not medical advice. Every unit is
different; this checklist reflects common practices, so always double-check your
own facility's policy. Made by Asdale Dynamics, LLC.*

Print this page once per swap. Check boxes with a pen — it's a card, not homework.

---

## Step 1 — Before You Ask Anyone

- ☐ Read (or re-skim) your unit's swap/change-of-shift policy — find it once, note where it lives.
- ☐ Note any rules that matter: notice period, [#] days before schedule posts, per-pay-period limits.
- ☐ Check *your* account: no pending attendance flags, no overtime already scheduled.
- ☐ Write the exact shifts down: date, shift, hours (e.g., [Tue 3/5, 1900-0700]).

## Step 2 — When You Ask a Colleague

- ☐ They are the same role / approved to work that shift and area (float and skill-mix rules!).
- ☐ Their hours stay within overtime rules after the swap.
- ☐ The trade is even, or they clearly agree it doesn't need to be (differential differences?).
- ☐ You gave them a real out: "no stress if it doesn't work."

## Step 3 — After They Say Yes

- ☐ Confirmation in writing exists (email or text saved).
- ☐ They replied-all or otherwise *confirmed dates themselves* — not just "sure."
- ☐ Coverage math checked (see `coverage-math-one-pager.md`)
- ☐ Differential/pay implication considered and accepted by both.

## Step 4 — The Approval

- ☐ Manager / charge nurse / scheduling office asked per your unit's actual chain.
- ☐ **Written** approval received (email, scheduling app, or initials on the tracker card).
- ☐ Schedule updated — don't assume, verify after it posts.
- ☐ Tracker card filled out (see `swap-confirmation-tracker.md`).

## Step 5 — The Day Before Each Shift

- ☐ Sent one friendly reminder the day before *both* swapped shifts.
- ☐ Prepared for your new date like it's a normal scheduled shift.

---

## Rules that trip people up (the honest list)

| Trip-up | What it looks like | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| Verbal-only yes | Memory vs. memory two weeks later | Always follow up in writing |
| OT surprise | Swap pushes a colleague into overtime | Do the math *before* asking |
| Skill-mix mismatch | LPN covering an RN shift | Verify role eligibility first |
| Differential blind spot | Nights traded for days silently shifts pay one of you | Name it out loud, agree anyway |
| The "soft trade" | "You owe me one" with no date | Give the favor a date — that's the tracker card's job |

## The one-sentence version

**Ask with specifics, confirm in writing, get it approved before it's real,
and track it so kindness doesn't get forgotten.**
