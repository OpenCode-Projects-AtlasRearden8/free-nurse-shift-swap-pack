# Thank-You Coupons (For the Colleague Who Bails You Out)

*Education and organization only — this pack is not medical advice — and these
coupons are definitely not an HR policy. They are personal gifts between coworkers.
Made with gratitude by Asdale Dynamics, LLC.*

Print, cut, and hand one over with the sentence we all remember: **"You're a
lifesaver."** One coupon = one clearly-worded kindness. Standard terms: no expiration,
redeem with the chef (you), smiles guaranteed at redemption.

## How to hand one over (20 seconds of courage)

1. Say the sentence: *"You really saved me — here's an IOU with fancy borders."*
2. Hand it over face up.
3. Let the awkward beat land; that's where the friendship lives.

## Ground rules (the un-fine print)

- Coupons are **personal gestures between coworkers** — they carry no authority over
  schedules, patient care, or assignments. "First swap priority" means *priority of ask*,
  and every actual swap still needs your unit's normal approvals.
- Nothing on this page replaces facility policy, HR guidance, or the law.
- Keep it kind, keep it optional, keep it fun.
