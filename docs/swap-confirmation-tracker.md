*Education and organization only — this pack is not medical advice. Track what your
own facility's policy requires. Made by Asdale Dynamics, LLC.*

One card per swap. The magic combo is **kind ask → written yes → approved → tracked** —
this card is the "tracked" part, so nobody ever relives "wait, I thought you covered me?"

## Quarter-fold legend (what each line means, if someone else finds it)

- **agrees in writing** — a text *or* email that states both dates out loud.
- **coverage math** — the three checks from `coverage-math-one-pager.md`.
- **manager approval (written)** — not "they said it's probably fine."
- **schedule shows the change** — the schedule itself is the only source of truth.
- **coupons** — from `thank-you-coupons.md`, redeemable by heroes.

## Two tips that save friendships

1. **Reminders are kindness, not distrust.** "See you tomorrow for our swap —
   thanks again!" protects *both* calendars.
2. **Close the loop out loud.** When their date arrives and everything holds,
   tell them it went well. Closed loops get traded with again.
