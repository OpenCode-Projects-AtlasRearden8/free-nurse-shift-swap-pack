# Coverage Math One-Pager (The Swap "Does This Balance?" Sheet)

*Education and organization only — this pack is not medical advice. Your facility's
own staffing and float rules always override anything on this page. Made by

## The whole idea in one sentence

**A swap is safe when it moves hours around without changing the totals —
same team hours, every shift still at minimum staffing, nobody pushed into overtime.**

## The Three Checks (do them in this order)

### Check 1 — Hours balance (zero-sum test)

Total hours given by Person A must equal hours received by Person A, and the same
for Person B. The team's total doesn't move — just the labels change.

```
A gives: Tue 1900-0700  = 12.0 h        A gets:  Fri 1900-0700 = 12.0 h
B gives: Fri 1900-0700  = 12.0 h        B gets:  Tue 1900-0700 = 12.0 h
                                              Team total change: 0.0 h  ✓
```

### Check 2 — Every single shift stays staffed

Does each *individual* shift still meet your unit's minimum headcount and skill mix?
Borrow the math your scheduler already uses:

| Shift | Min needed | Before swap | After swap | Okay? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tue 1900-0700 | 5 | 5 | 5 (B swapped in for A) | ✓ |
| Fri 1900-0700 | 5 | 5 | 5 (A swapped in for B) | ✓ |

### Check 3 — No one tips into overtime

Map the swap onto the pay period:

```
Pay period: [Oct 6 – Oct 19]
A's hours after swap: [72.0]  (limit-ish: watch seniority/OT-prone balances)
B's hours after swap: [72.0]
Both under facility overtime threshold for the period:  ✓
```

## Percent-safe quick reference (percent of *your own* weekly cap)

Per shift of 12 h on a 3 × 12 h week: **12 h = 33.3% of your week.**
So a straight one-for-one 12-for-12 swap moves exactly 33.3% of one week's
workload from you to them, and 33.3% back. Balanced swaps never move more.

(`33.3` avoids the rounding lie of `33` — two swapped shifts are 66.7%, not 66%.)

## Red flags (said "no" to yourself before asking)

- 🚩 The swap gives one person a **78-hour** two-week stretch.
- 🚩 One of the two shifts drops below minimum *or* skill mix (e.g., no charge-capable RN).
- 🚩 "We'll figure out the return shift later" — that's not a swap, that's a debt.
- 🚩 A weekend/rotation fairness promise ("you take all my weekends forever") with no date.
- 🚩 Anyone claims "it's fine, nobody checks" — someone always checks.

## What break-even *is not*

- ❌ "I'll trade 12 straight hours for 8 shady ones plus a favor." The math is the contract.
- ❌ Swapping a *holiday* for a *regular day* without extra agreement — holidays usually
  carry their own fairness rules; treat them as separate negotiations.
- ❌ Quietly changing differentials (night ↔ day) without both people acknowledging the pay shift.

## Pocket summary

> **Zero-sum for people. Non-negative for staffing. Nobody into overtime.**
> If all three hold, ask with confidence. If one doesn't hold, don't ask — rework it.
