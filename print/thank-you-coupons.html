How to hand one over (20 seconds of courage)

  1. Say the sentence: “You really saved me — here's an IOU with fancy borders.”
  2. Hand it over face up.
  3. Let the awkward beat land; that's where the friendship lives.
Ground rules (the un-fine print): coupons are personal gestures between coworkers — they carry no authority over schedules, patient care, or assignments. “First swap priority” means priority of ask, and every actual swap still needs your unit's normal approvals. Nothing here replaces facility policy, HR guidance, or the law. Keep it kind, keep it optional, keep it fun.
