Unit-Approval Checklist (Your Swap Paper Trail)

Print this page once per swap. Check boxes with a pen — it's a card, not homework.

Step 1 — Before You Ask Anyone

  • Read (or re-skim) your unit's swap policy — find it once, note where it lives.
  • Note any rules that matter: notice period, days before schedule posts, per-pay-period limits.
  • Check your account: no pending flags, no overtime already scheduled.
  • Write the exact shifts down: date, shift, hours (e.g., [Tue 3/5, 1900-0700]).

Step 2 — When You Ask a Colleague

  • They are the same role / approved to work that shift and area (float and skill-mix rules apply).
  • Their hours stay within overtime rules after the swap.
  • The trade is even, or they clearly agree it doesn't need to be (differential differences?).
  • You gave them a real out: “no stress if it doesn't work.”

Step 3 — After They Say Yes

  • Confirmation in writing exists (email or saved text).
  • They confirmed the dates themselves — not just “sure.”
  • Coverage math checked (three checks pass).
  • Differential / pay implication considered and accepted by both.

Step 4 — The Approval

  • Manager / charge nurse / scheduling office asked per your unit's actual chain.
  • Written approval received (email, scheduling app, or initials).
  • Requested the schedule change and will verify after it posts — don't assume.
  • Tracker card filled out.

Step 5 — The Day Before Each Shift

  • Sent one friendly reminder the day before both swapped shifts.
  • Prepared for the new date like it's a normal scheduled shift.
